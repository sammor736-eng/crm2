When to call

Look for water damage and recurring moisture.

Water stains, peeling paint, a musty odor, and visible discoloration can all point to a moisture problem. In Brooklyn apartments and brownstones, leaks from plumbing, roofs, and neighboring units may affect hidden spaces behind walls or ceilings.

Visible mold is a reason to investigate the source. A smell alone does not identify the cause or extent; an inspection helps establish what is happening.

If an HPD violation is involved, let us know when you call so the scope can account for the moisture concern and needed documentation.

Common questions

Brooklyn mold removal questions

How much does mold removal cost in Brooklyn?

The existing page gives a broad $500–$6,000 range. Actual cost depends on the size of the area, access, materials, and the work needed to control moisture. We can give you a project-specific estimate after reviewing the property.

How long does mold removal take?

The existing page describes most projects as taking one to five days, with larger or more complex work taking longer. The schedule depends on the affected area and drying or repair needs.

What causes mold in Brooklyn homes?

Excess moisture from leaks, flooding, high indoor humidity, or poor ventilation is a common cause. Basements and older building assemblies can make the source harder to spot.

Can I clean a small area myself?

Small surface spots may be manageable, but recurring growth, water-damaged material, or a larger affected area calls for a closer assessment. Addressing the moisture source matters as much as cleaning the visible surface.

Do you handle water damage as well?

Yes. Prime Mold Removal also responds to water damage and can discuss drying, cleanup, and restoration needs.
